## 👋 About

DevOps and platform engineer with **5+ years** building the CI/CD pipelines, cloud infrastructure, and Kubernetes platforms that keep production systems fast and reliable — cutting deployment times by up to **70%** and eliminating recurring incidents at scale.

As **Technical Lead at Valyria Visions**, I lead AI/ML, cloud, and drone operations — building **PhenoLens**, a drone- and ML-driven phenotyping platform for precision agriculture. I like the hard part: making models, data pipelines, and physical drone systems run as one production-grade whole.

## 🔭 What I'm building now

- 🌾 **PhenoLens** — drone + machine-learning phenotyping for precision, sustainable agriculture
- 💻 **Development Solutions** — end-to-end mobile apps, web platforms, and e-commerce for digital transformation
- ☸️ **Kubernetes** — open-source contributor to container-orchestration improvements
- 📈 Going deeper on MLOps pipelines and geospatial AI
